# Provenance and attribution

The period-two forest and sharp S^3 von Mises--Fisher transport/Poincare
inputs are adopted from Deep Bhattacharjee and Ushashi Bhattacharya,
creelie/4Y-M, at the immutable revision
[b2d359f1197f9f50aae8f6890edb6d9106531fa3](https://github.com/creelie/4Y-M/tree/b2d359f1197f9f50aae8f6890edb6d9106531fa3).

Primary inputs:

- [Forest manuscript](https://github.com/creelie/4Y-M/blob/b2d359f1197f9f50aae8f6890edb6d9106531fa3/strong-coupling/forest/main.tex): direction field, gauge identity, block integration scheme.
- [PRD manuscript](https://github.com/creelie/4Y-M/blob/b2d359f1197f9f50aae8f6890edb6d9106531fa3/strong-coupling/prd/main.tex): sharp chordal quarter and auxiliary Poincare inputs.
- [Upstream MIT license](https://github.com/creelie/4Y-M/blob/b2d359f1197f9f50aae8f6890edb6d9106531fa3/LICENSE), reproduced in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

The forest geometry is independently enumerated here; its adopted table
and proof exposition retain the upstream attribution. The active-link
coloring, projected exact-elimination response estimate, variance
sharpening and separate outward rational implementation were developed
during this project. No upstream certificate executable is imported.
Novelty and priority have not been established.

A later inspected revision,
[b409102e072485d67fbd3507bceaf6ff9d4a924b](https://github.com/creelie/4Y-M/tree/b409102e072485d67fbd3507bceaf6ff9d4a924b),
credits Deep Bhattacharjee, Ushashi Bhattacharya and James Montgomery
Lockwood. It reports a 0.345 SU(2) three-link certificate. The present
0.385 estimate is for a different conditional specification after an
independent color has been integrated out. Comparing the endpoints
alone does not compare every theorem hypothesis or establish priority.

The positive physical Wilson transfer construction is classical:
[M. Luscher, Construction of a selfadjoint, strictly positive transfer
matrix for Euclidean lattice gauge theories, DESY 76/54](https://bib-pubdb1.desy.de/record/396349/files/7611148.pdf?version=1).
The release's transfer note supplies the free-end exhaustion and dense
observable spectral argument appropriate to this certificate.

This package was prepared with AI assistance, including separate agent

not independent human review. No Lean proof of the full result is claimed.
